MechReady privacy policy

MechReady is provided by Hanne. The app works without an account and does not send your answers or results to us. It has no in-app ads or analytics.

Data on your device

Your practice and mock exam results, the date of each attempt, and your language choice are stored on your device.

Clear MechReady’s app storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to delete all saved results.

Contact and support

Email support@hannelife.com for privacy questions or requests. If you contact us, we use the information you choose to send to respond and keep it as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations.
